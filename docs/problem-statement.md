# Problem Statement

## Background

Modern global supply chains operate across highly interconnected multi-modal transport networks involving ocean liners, air freighters, rail corridors, and trucking fleets. When critical hubs suffer unexpected disruption—such as berth fires, severe weather cyclones, or port worker strikes—the impact cascades far beyond the primary point of failure. Logistics managers must navigate multi-node dependencies across international borders, strict delivery schedules, and complex temperature requirements for bio-pharma and perishable goods.

## The Problem

When a major logistics hub fails (such as a warehouse fire closing Berths 1–8 at Mumbai Port Trust), operations teams spend 24 to 72 hours manually auditing manifests across disconnected systems. During this critical window:
- **Stranded Cargo & Risk Exposure:** Tens of millions of dollars in cargo ($78.845M across 20 affected shipments) sit stranded without automated recovery routing.
- **Cold-Chain Spoilage Risk:** Perishable foods and temperature-sensitive pharmaceuticals (e.g. COVID vaccines, oncology drugs, frozen chicken) suffer thermal excursions, risking complete product loss.
- **Cascading Financial Impact:** Demurrage fees, late delivery penalties, and factory line shutdowns escalate to $127M in total network financial impact.
- **Fleet Under-Utilisation:** Available fleet assets hover at a wasteful 52.9% average utilisation because dispatchers lack real-time visibility into unallocated vessel, truck, rail, and air capacity nearby.

## Who is Affected

- **Logistics Control Tower Managers & Dispatchers:** Responsible for real-time fleet operations, vessel routing, and incident response.
- **Cold-Chain Compliance Officers:** Managing temperature-sensitive bio-pharma and refrigerated food shipments requiring immediate excursion remediation.
- **Supply Chain Directors & Enterprise Executives:** Need rapid quantitative risk clarity ($78.845M value at risk) to approve capital-intensive recovery decisions (e.g., chartering air freight or rerouting container ships).

## Why It Matters

- **Financial Impact:** Unmitigated port closures can cost enterprises over $100M in cascading supply chain penalties and lost sales.
- **Public Health & Safety:** Temperature excursions in bio-pharma shipments destroy critical vaccine batches and oncology therapeutics.
- **Operational Waste:** Operating fleets at 52.9% utilisation inflates fuel consumption, increases carbon footprints, and squanders millions in transport assets.

## Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

1. **Fragmented Legacy TMS:** Traditional Transport Management Systems provide static, isolated alerts without automated cascade analysis (connecting port failure → shipment → route → fleet asset → cold-chain risk → financial exposure).
2. **Manual Spreadsheet Redeployment:** Mathematical optimization across multiple vehicles, capacities, priority levels, and thermal constraints cannot be calculated manually under time pressure.
3. **Lack of Scenario Simulation:** Executive planners must commit millions to rerouting decisions without quantitative tools to test "What-If" trade-offs (e.g. comparing $1.2M JNPT reroute cost against delay reduction).
